## System Reference Document

The spell and class data in `src/data/srd-2014.json` and `src/data/srd-2024.json` is taken from:

- **System Reference Document 5.1** by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document
- **System Reference Document 5.2** by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document

Both are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

The data has been modified: trimmed to the fields the app needs, reformatted, and for SRD 5.2 the damage scaling tables have been derived from the spell descriptions. The app shows the full attribution in its "Settings and data" book and a short one in the footer of every view.

## 5e-database

The SRD data was obtained in JSON form from the [5e-bits/5e-database](https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database) project, licensed under the MIT Licence.

## Class emblems

The class emblems on the book covers are original drawings made for this project. They are not artwork from Wizards of the Coast or D&D Beyond, and are covered by the MIT Licence together with the code.

## Test fixtures

`tests/fixtures/` contains an invented class ("Tinkerer") and an invented spell ("Pocket Spark") used only to test the import feature. They are not game content.
